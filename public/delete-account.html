Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: 2026

This page explains how to delete your Scribe account and the data associated with it. An account is optional in Scribe — if you never signed in, you have no account to delete, and your sermons live only on your device.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Scribe on your phone.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This permanently removes your authentication record (your email/sign-in) from our authentication provider (Supabase).

Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email support@scribehq.app from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We will process the request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record — email address and any profile fields (display name, role, church) held by our authentication provider. Deleted permanently.
  • Your sermons, transcripts, outlines, and audio — these are stored only on your device, never on our servers. They are removed when you delete them in the app or uninstall Scribe.

What we keep

  • Nothing tied to your identity is retained after account deletion. We do not operate a server that stores your sermon content.
