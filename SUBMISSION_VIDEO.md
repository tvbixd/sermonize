# Scribe — Submission Video

A ready-to-record script for an AI-competition submission. Built as a **2-minute
demo** (the sweet spot for most competitions), with a **60-second cut** marked so
you can trim to a hard limit. If your competition specifies a length, tell me and
I'll retime it exactly.

**One-line pitch:** *Scribe records a sermon and hands the preacher back a clean,
cited outline — automatically.*

**Tone:** calm, confident, real. You built this; let it speak. Show the product
doing the work more than you talking about it.

---

## Before you record

- **Screen recording** is your best footage. On iPhone: Settings → Control Center
  → add Screen Recording, then swipe down and tap it. Record a real (short) run:
  tap record, speak a 20–30s mini-sermon that quotes 2–3 verses out loud, stop,
  and let the outline generate. Capture the whole thing in one take.
- Speak your demo verses **clearly and by name** — e.g. "Turn with me to *Romans
  chapter 8, verse 28*" — so the scripture detection has something clean to catch
  on camera.
- Record **face/voiceover audio separately** with the phone close to your mouth
  (or a lav mic). Don't rely on room audio.
- Grab **one clean shot of the app icon** on your home screen and **one of the
  finished outline** scrolled top to bottom.
- Shoot in **portrait** if the competition plays on phones; **landscape** if it's
  a projector/screen. When unsure, landscape 1080p.

## The 2-minute script

Timecodes are cumulative. `[VO]` = voiceover, `[SCREEN]` = what's on screen,
`[TEXT]` = on-screen caption. **★60s cut** = keep only the starred beats for a
one-minute version.

### 0:00–0:10 — Hook ★
- **[SCREEN]** Black, then the Scribe wordmark fades in. Cut to a hand holding the
  phone at a pulpit / desk.
- **[VO]** "Every week, millions of people preach a sermon they spent days
  preparing… and then have nothing clean to keep."
- **[TEXT]** *Preach it. Keep it.*

### 0:10–0:25 — The problem ★
- **[SCREEN]** Quick shots: a messy handwritten note, a phone voice-memo list, a
  blank document.
- **[VO]** "The talk is live and then it's gone. No structured notes. No reliable
  record. And every verse you quoted has to be hunted down and typed up by hand."

### 0:25–0:35 — Meet Scribe ★
- **[SCREEN]** App icon → open Scribe → the clean record screen.
- **[VO]** "Scribe fixes that. One tap, and it listens while you preach."
- **[TEXT]** *Scribe — AI notes for preachers*

### 0:35–1:05 — The demo (the core) ★
- **[SCREEN]** Screen recording: tap **Record**. The recording indicator pulses.
  You speak (we hear your real demo audio, lowered under VO):
  *"...and Paul reminds us in Romans chapter 8, verse 28, that all things work
  together for good…"*
- **[SCREEN]** As you say it, the **scripture surfaces on screen** — Romans 8:28,
  with the verse text. Let the viewer *see* it appear.
- **[VO]** "As you speak, Scribe transcribes every word — and the moment you cite
  a verse, it detects it and looks it up for you. Even spoken plainly, like
  'Matthew chapter twelve, verse twenty-four.'"

### 1:05–1:30 — The payoff ★
- **[SCREEN]** Tap **Stop**. Brief "generating" beat, then the finished **outline
  animates in**: Title, Theme, Summary, numbered Points, cited scriptures. Slowly
  scroll it top to bottom.
- **[VO]** "When you stop, the whole message is already organized — a title, the
  theme, a summary, your main points, and every scripture, cited in your
  translation. You walk away with finished notes, not a wall of transcript."
- **[TEXT]** *record → transcribe → outline → cite*

### 1:30–1:45 — Why it matters
- **[SCREEN]** Split or quick cuts: the translation picker (show "200+"), the
  export options (PDF / Markdown), the sermon saved in the library.
- **[VO]** "It supports over two hundred Bible translations, works on iOS and
  Android, keeps your sermons on your device, and it's free to preach with —
  built to run at effectively no cost."

### 1:45–2:00 — Close ★
- **[SCREEN]** Back to the Scribe wordmark on a clean ground.
- **[VO]** "Scribe. The message you preached — finally kept."
- **[TEXT]** *Scribe · scribehq.app*  (use your real URL)

## Editing notes

- **Music:** one soft, warm bed under the whole thing (piano/pad). Duck it under
  the VO. Silence it for a beat when the first verse surfaces on screen — let that
  moment land.
- **Pace:** hold on the "verse surfaces" and "outline appears" moments a little
  longer than feels natural. Those two beats *are* the product.
- **Captions:** burn in subtitles — many competition entries are watched muted.
- **Honesty:** everything in this script is true of the shipped app. Don't claim
  user numbers or accuracy percentages you can't back up; "tested through real
  church services" is your credibility, and it's real.
- **End card:** hold the final wordmark + URL for a full 2–3 seconds so judges can
  note it.
